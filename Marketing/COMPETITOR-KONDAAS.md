## Who they are built for
- **Main money:** factories and mills in the Coimbatore-Tirupur textile belt, colleges, IT parks. MW-scale. Their case studies are all industrial.
- **Second:** Kerala and TN homes (KSEB and TANGEDCO content).
- **Third:** dealers and franchise owners (dealership, franchise and expo forms) - they are building a network across South India.
- **Trust levers:** age (1995), customer count, named client projects with sizes, "guaranteed performance or we make up the units", app monitoring, zero-cost EMI, refer-and-earn.

So their site sells **scale and track record to businesses**. Our homes-first buyer is different, but for the new commercial push, their playbook shows what business buyers look for.

## What works (worth learning from)
1. **Named project proof** - "Soundar Textiles 1.8 MW", "Periyar University 300 kW". Business buyers trust exact size + client type + year more than anything.
2. **Industry segmentation** - Manufacturing, Textile, Education, Offices, each with its own block and projects. A factory owner sees "people like me".
3. **Clear split in the menu**: For Homes vs Commercial. Business visitors find their page in one tap.
4. **Offers beyond the panel**: EMI financing, performance guarantee, monitoring app, referral rewards.
5. **Volume of guides** for every state process (KSEB, TANGEDCO, PM Surya Ghar).

## What is weak (where we beat them)
1. **Broken mobile layout** - headings and hero text run off the right edge of a phone screen ("Commerci...", "Best..."). Our pages fit cleanly.
2. **Mostly stock photos** (sunset solar fields, factory renders). We use real Cuddalore installs.
3. **Thin, far-away location pages** (Delhi, Jaipur, Hyderabad) - looks like reach for SEO, risky as doorway pages. Ours are real local pages with local facts.
4. **No prices on the commercial page**, generic "Reach Us" forms with no qualification. We show market price ranges and a quick checker.
5. **Big empty whitespace, long scroll** (~12,000px on mobile for the commercial page).
6. "Best in India" style titles - broad and hard to rank. Ours are town-specific.

## Adopt / Adapt / Avoid

| Idea | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Separate **Commercial** landing page + menu link | **Adopt** | We have no landing page for business buyers; only a blog post. |
| **Industry blocks** (who we power) | **Adapt** to our area | Their textile-mill list does not fit Cuddalore. Use our local mix: SIPCOT Cuddalore units, Panruti cashew processing, rice mills, hospitals and clinics, schools and colleges, hotels and lodges in Puducherry, marriage halls, shops. |
| **Named projects with size and year** | **Adopt - needs owner data** | Strongest trust signal for business buyers. Only real projects, client named only with permission. |
| Apartment / RWA page | **Adopt** (they don't have one) | Gap in their site and ours; ₹18,000/kW RWA subsidy is a strong hook. |
| Product catalogue pages (inverter models) | **Avoid for now** | Low search demand for a local installer; hard to keep accurate. |
| 40 location pages including other states | **Avoid** | Doorway risk; we only serve our region. Grow towns slowly where we really serve. |
| "Guaranteed units or we pay" | **Owner decision** | Powerful, but only if Surya's Solar can honour it in writing. |
| EMI / zero-cost financing | **Owner decision** | Only if there is a real bank or NBFC tie-up. |
| Dealer / franchise forms | **Avoid** | Not our model. |
| Red/black heavy design, huge whitespace | **Avoid** | Our premium-simple blue look tests better for trust and scroll length. |

## Our plan for commercial and apartment orders

**1. /commercial-solar landing page** (Cuddalore-first, all TN)
- Hero: "Rooftop solar for businesses in Cuddalore and Puducherry"; form left (business type, monthly EB bill in ₹, roof type, town, mobile).
- Industries we power (local list above), cost per kW table (₹40,000-55,000 per kW for 20 kW+, market range), 40% accelerated depreciation, CAPEX vs OPEX, payback example, process, FAQ schema.
- Target queries: commercial solar Cuddalore, industrial rooftop solar Tamil Nadu, solar for factory, solar for hospital/school, commercial solar Puducherry.

**2. /apartment-solar landing page** (RWA / builders)
- ₹18,000 per kW RWA subsidy for common areas, what it powers (lifts, pumps, lights), an "RWA meeting" one-page summary the secretary can forward on WhatsApp, enquiry form for the association.

**3. Projects page** - real commercial and apartment jobs with photos, size, type, town, year.

**4. Header menu** - add a "For Business" link next to the home-buyer links.

**5. Off-site** - Google Business Profile: add commercial services and post business project photos; WhatsApp one-pager for walking into SIPCOT units, cashew units, schools and hospitals.

## Facts needed from the owner before building
1. Your biggest commercial/industrial jobs: size (kW), type of business, town, year, photos. Can we name the client?
2. Any apartment / RWA installs done?
3. Largest system you can take on (50 kW? 100 kW? 500 kW? HT connections?).
4. EMI / bank loan tie-up or OPEX (pay-per-unit) option available?
5. Do you offer an AMC / maintenance contract for businesses? Price?
6. Who should business enquiries go to (same number or a separate one)?
