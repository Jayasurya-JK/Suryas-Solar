## Tamil pages: research (18 Oct 2026)

- **Tamil-script searches exist but are much smaller than English**: about 300 distinct Tamil-script solar searches vs about 13,000 in English. Most Tamil Nadu people (even Tamil speakers) search solar in English.
- **Buying intent in Tamil is real**: "சோலார் பேனல் விலை" (price), "சோலார் பேனல் மானியம் பெறுவது எப்படி" (how to get subsidy), "3 கிலோவாட் சோலார் பேனல் விலை", "மின் கட்டணம் கணக்கிடும் முறை" (EB bill calculation), "solar maniyam apply online".
- **Tanglish is weak** (about 56 suggestions): people typing in English letters use English words.
- **Our Search Console**: only about 280 Tamil-related impressions in 16 months, because we have no Tamil pages yet.
- **Competition in Tamil is thin**: few quality Tamil solar pages exist, so a good page can rank quickly.
- **Value beyond Google**: Tamil pages are easy to share on WhatsApp with parents and older family members, who often decide on home spending.

**Decision:** build a **small, separate Tamil section** (not mixed with English) at /ta/, with 5 high-intent pages: subsidy (மானியம்), price (விலை), 3kW, EB bill calculation with a Tamil EB calculator, and how to apply. Each page links to its English twin (hreflang). Owner (native speaker) reviews the Tamil before publishing. Measure for 8 weeks; expand only if Tamil impressions and enquiries grow.
