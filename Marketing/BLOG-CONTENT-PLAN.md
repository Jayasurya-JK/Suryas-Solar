## 4. End-to-end workflow for every post

1. **Plan** - pick next row; check no existing post targets it (`solar-content-writer` skill, step 1).
2. **Research** - latest Search Console queries for the topic; verify any fact/subsidy/date online; facts from `suryas-business-facts`.
3. **Write** - answer-first, one table, local proof, FAQ (4-6), 3-6 internal links, CTA (`solar-content-writer` skill).
4. **Image** - Claude gives filename + Gemini/ChatGPT prompt; owner generates and drops the PNG/JPG in `public/images/`; Claude converts to webp. Real photos preferred; never fake "our installation" photos.
5. **Validate** - `check_posts.js`, NAP check, `npm run build` (`new-blog-post` skill).
6. **Approve** - owner reviews on localhost for anything new in design; text-only posts can ship directly unless owner wants to read first.
7. **Publish** - commit + push (Vercel auto-deploys), verify live (`ship-site` skill).
8. **Index** - Search Console "Request indexing" (max ~10/day).
9. **Promote** - same day: Google Business Profile post, WhatsApp status, Instagram carousel text (Claude writes all three).
10. **Measure** - after 4 weeks: impressions, position, CTR per post; rewrite title if CTR < 1%; mark result in the tracker below.

**Cadence:** 3 posts per week (Mon / Wed / Fri) = 30 posts in 10 weeks. Image generation is the main bottleneck - Claude sends prompts for a whole sprint at once so the owner can batch them.
