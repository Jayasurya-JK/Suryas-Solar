# Image Prompts - All Blog Posts (Oct 2026)

One prompt per post for **Gemini (Nano Banana)** or **ChatGPT**. Until you make an image, each post shows a placeholder photo from the site, so nothing is broken. Replace them at your own pace, most important first.

## How to use

1. Copy the prompt (the text in the grey box) into Gemini or ChatGPT.
2. Generate 2-3 options, pick the most natural-looking one.
3. Save it as **PNG or JPG** into `Web/public/images/` with the **exact filename** shown (any extension).
4. Tell Claude "images added". Claude converts to webp, puts it on the post and deploys.

**Rules for every image**
- 16:9 landscape, 1200 x 675 or larger.
- No text, numbers or logos inside the image (AI text is often wrong, and Google cannot read it).
- No other companies' brand names or logos.
- Never present an AI image as a real customer's house or "our installation". Real photos of your work are always better - send those too.
- Tamil Nadu look: flat white concrete terraces, coconut trees, bright sky, everyday Indian clothing.

**Priority order:** A = money / high-traffic posts (do first), B = supporting posts, C = when you have time.
