**Short answer:** with the full **₹1 lakh subsidy** (₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar + ₹22,000 Tamil Nadu top-up), rooftop solar in Tamil Nadu pays back in about **2.5-3.5 years** for homes with **two-month bills above about ₹5,000**, around **4-5 years** for bills near **₹3,000-3,500**, and **8+ years** for **small bills under ₹1,500**. After that, the system keeps saving for 20+ more years.

We show the real slab-based math below, because many quotes promise "2-year payback" for everyone. That is not true for every home.

## Why Bill Size Changes Everything

Tamil Nadu's tariff is **telescopic and cheap at the bottom**:

*   Homes using **500 units or less** (two months) get the **first 200 units free** and pay about ₹4.50-6.00 for the rest.
*   Above **500 units**, only 100 units are free, and the slabs climb to **₹8, ₹9, ₹10 and ₹11 per unit**.

So each solar unit saves you **₹4.50** in a small home but **₹9-11** in a big AC home. The same 3kW system is worth **almost twice as much** to a home on the expensive slabs. This is why AC homes get the fastest payback. See [average electricity bill by home size](/blog/2026-10-17-average-electricity-bill-tamil-nadu-by-home-size).

## Worked Example: 3BHK With One AC, 900 Units per Two Months

*   **Bill without solar:** about **₹5,650** every two months (about ₹2,825 a month).
*   **3kW solar** produces about 720-900 units per two months.
*   **Bill with solar:** close to the **fixed charge** for most of the year.
*   **Yearly saving:** about **₹33,000**.
*   **Net cost:** ₹1.8-2.0 lakh market price minus ₹1 lakh subsidy = **₹80,000-1,00,000**.
*   **Payback:** **about 2.5-3 years**.
*   **25-year savings:** roughly **₹7.5-8.5 lakh** at today's tariffs, after allowing for panel ageing and one inverter replacement. If tariffs rise, savings rise too.

## What Makes Payback Faster or Slower

| Faster payback | Slower payback |
| :--- | :--- |
| High bill (500+ units per two months) | Small bill, mostly in the free slab |
| Full ₹1 lakh subsidy (registered from 5 Aug 2026) | Missed the Tamil Nadu top-up or the central subsidy |
| Shade-free, south-facing roof, raised structure | Shade from trees, tanks or walls |
| Daytime use (work from home, AC in afternoon) | Most use at night with no net meter benefit |
| Correct system size | Over-sized system for a small bill |
| Quality install (low losses, no downtime) | Cheap install with frequent faults |

**The biggest lever is size.** An over-sized system for a small bill wastes money. We size every system from your last six bills.

## Without the Tamil Nadu Top-up

If you registered on PM Surya Ghar **before 5 August 2026**, or the state cap is used up, you get only the central ₹78,000. Your net cost rises by **₹22,000**, and payback grows by roughly **4-12 months** depending on your bill. For AC homes it still pays back in about **3-5 years**. For small bills, consider waiting until your usage grows.
