**Short answer:** most high EB bills in Chennai come from **crossing 500 units in a two-month cycle**, usually because the **AC runs for long hours in Chennai's heat and humidity**. Above 500 units you lose the extra free units and pay **₹8-11 per unit** on the higher slabs, so a small rise in use can **double the bill**. Other common causes are estimated readings, old appliances, water pumps and wiring leakage. Tariffs are the same as the rest of Tamil Nadu; usage is what differs.

---

## The Slab Cliff: Why Bills Jump Suddenly

Tamil Nadu domestic tariffs are the same in Chennai and every other district. For a two-month cycle (approximate rates):

| Two-month units | Approx. bill | What changed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 400 | about ₹1,000 | 200 units free |
| 480 | about ₹1,480 | Still under 500 |
| 550 | about ₹2,450 | **Crossed 500: only 100 units free, ₹8 slab starts** |
| 750 | about ₹4,250 | ₹9 slab |
| 1,000 | about ₹6,700 | ₹10 slab |

Going from **480 to 550 units** (about 15% more electricity) increases the bill by about **65%**. Full slab math: [TNEB slab rates 2026](/blog/2026-04-30-how-to-calculate-eb-bill-tamil-nadu-examples).

## 9 Common Causes of a High Bill in Chennai

### 1. AC hours in humid weather
Chennai's humidity makes nights uncomfortable, so ACs run longer, often 8-10 hours. One 1.5 ton AC can use **300-480 units per two months** in summer. See [how many units an AC uses](/blog/2026-04-29-how-many-units-does-ac-consume-per-day-india).

### 2. Low AC temperature settings
Running the AC at 18-20°C makes the compressor work much harder than at 24-26°C.

### 3. Old, low-star appliances
A 10-15-year-old refrigerator or a non-inverter AC can use 30-50% more power than a modern 5-star model.

### 4. Water pumps and sumps
Many Chennai homes pump water from sumps to overhead tanks. A faulty float valve or a pump left on can waste many units.

### 5. Geysers and induction cooking
A water heater left on, or daily induction cooking, adds units quickly.

### 6. Estimated (not actual) meter readings
If the reading was estimated, one cycle can be under-billed and the next over-billed. Check the bill for an "estimated" note and compare with your meter.

### 7. Wiring leakage or earth faults
Old wiring can leak current. If your meter runs with everything switched off, call an electrician.

### 8. More people at home
Work-from-home, holidays or guests can push a home over 500 units without anyone noticing.

### 9. Summer timing
April-June cycles are always the highest. Read [why the EB bill is high in summer](/blog/2026-04-27-why-is-electricity-bill-high-in-summer-tamil-nadu).

## How to Check Your Bill Is Correct

1.  **Read your meter** and compare it with the "present reading" on the bill.
2.  **Subtract the previous reading** to get your units, and check the cycle length is about 60 days.
3.  **Recalculate** with the slabs above (add the fixed charge).
4.  **Look for "estimated"** or unusual readings.
5.  If it is wrong, **raise a complaint with TNPDCL** with a photo of the meter and the bill.

## Quick Fixes That Cut the Bill

| Fix | Typical effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| AC at 24-26°C with a ceiling fan | Saves about 5-6% of AC power per degree |
| Sleep mode / timer on the AC | Cuts 1-2 hours of early-morning running |
| Clean AC filters every 2 weeks in summer | Restores efficiency |
| Timer or automatic controller on water pump | Stops overflow waste |
| BLDC fans and LED lights | Half the power of old fans and tube lights |
| Replace a very old fridge | Can save 15-30 units a month |

These can keep a home just under the **500-unit cliff**, which is where the biggest savings are.

## Apartment vs Independent House: Why Bills Differ

| | Apartment flat | Independent house |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Water pumping | Usually on the common bill (maintenance) | On your own bill |
| Heat gain | Top-floor flats get the hottest; middle floors stay cooler | Roof heat enters directly unless insulated |
| Typical extra load | ACs in more rooms, smaller balconies | Pumps, outdoor lights, geysers |
| Solar option | Association installs on the common terrace | Install rooftop solar directly |

A simple fix for top-floor flats and independent houses is **heat-reflective (white) roof coating**, which lowers indoor temperature and AC hours. Combined with solar on the same roof, it cuts bills from both sides.
