**Short answer:** a "25-year solar panel warranty" is usually a **performance warranty**: the manufacturer promises the panel will still give a set share of its rated power, often **80-87% after 25-30 years**. Manufacturing defects are covered by a separate **product warranty, typically 10-15 years**. The **inverter** has its own **5-10 year** warranty, and the **installer's workmanship** warranty covers the structure, wiring and leaks. Knowing which warranty covers what saves you money and arguments later.

> *Terms vary by brand and model. Always read the warranty document for the exact panel model in your quote. General guide as of October 2026.*

## The 4 Warranties in a Solar System

| Warranty | Who gives it | Typical length | What it covers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Panel performance** | Panel maker | 25 - 30 years | Minimum power output over the years |
| **Panel product** | Panel maker | 10 - 15 years | Defects: delamination, junction box, frame, cells |
| **Inverter** | Inverter maker | 5 - 10 years (often extendable) | Inverter failure and faults |
| **Workmanship / installation** | Your installer | 1 - 5 years (varies) | Structure, wiring, earthing, roof leaks from installation |

Many buyers only hear "25 years" and assume everything is covered for 25 years. It is not, so get **all four in writing**.

## Performance Warranty: What "80% After 25 Years" Means

Every panel slowly loses output, called **degradation**. A typical modern warranty looks like this:

| Year | Guaranteed output (example, Mono PERC) | Guaranteed output (example, TOPCon) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Year 1 | about 98% | about 99% |
| Each later year | loses up to about 0.55% | loses up to about 0.4% |
| Year 25 | about 84% | about 89% |
| Year 30 | about 82% | about 87% |

*Illustrative figures; check the datasheet of your exact model. TOPCon panels usually carry a better degradation guarantee.*

**In units:** a 3kW system making 13 units a day in year one should still make about **11 units a day around year 25**. If your panels fall well below the guaranteed curve (after accounting for dust, shade and weather), that is a valid performance claim.

## Product Warranty: Defects You Can Claim

Covered (typical):
*   Delamination (layers separating) or bubbles inside the glass
*   Junction box or connector failure
*   Frame corrosion beyond normal limits
*   Cell cracks or hot spots from manufacturing defects
*   Glass breakage without any impact

Not covered (typical):
*   Damage from cyclones, lightning, falling coconuts or branches
*   Cracks from people walking on panels
*   Damage from wrong installation (drilling frames, wrong clamps)
*   Salt corrosion on a **structure** (that is the installer's area, not the panel maker's)

## Inverter Warranty

The inverter is the part most likely to fail in 25 years. A good inverter lasts **10-12 years**, so plan for **one replacement**. Check:
*   Warranty length and whether an **extension** is available
*   Is the warranty **replacement or repair**?
*   Does a **local service partner** exist in [Cuddalore](/service-areas/cuddalore), or must it be shipped?

## Workmanship Warranty: The One That Matters Locally

Most problems in coastal Tamil Nadu come from **installation**, not panels:
*   Rusted bolts and structures from salt air
*   Loose DC connectors causing heating
*   Water leaks where the structure is anchored
*   Poor earthing

Ask your installer for a **written workmanship warranty** and what it covers. ## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1.  **Keep the invoice** with every **panel serial number** listed (also needed for the subsidy).
2.  **Note the problem:** monitoring app data showing low units, photos of the damage, error codes.
3.  **Call your installer first.** They test the system and confirm it is a manufacturing issue, not dust or shade.
4.  **The installer raises the claim** with the manufacturer with test reports.
5.  **Replacement or repair**, depending on the warranty terms.

Tip: an installer who is an **authorised partner** of the brand can process claims faster. We are an official partner of TATA Power Solar and Exide.

## 6 Things That Void Your Warranty

| Mistake | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| Drilling holes in the panel frame | Breaks the frame and seal |
| Walking or standing on panels | Causes micro-cracks |
| Pressure washers or harsh chemicals | Damages the coating and seals |
| DIY or unqualified installation | Most makers need a trained installer |
| Serial numbers missing from invoice | No proof of purchase |
| Mixing panel models in one string | Can cause mismatch and hot spots |

## Add Insurance for Cyclones and Lightning

Warranties do not cover natural disasters. Add the solar system to your **home insurance** for storm, flood, lightning and theft. It costs little compared with the system value, and in Cuddalore's cyclone belt it is worth it.
