**Short answer:** a **2kW solar system produces about 8 to 10 units a day** in Tamil Nadu, which is **240 to 300 units a month** and **2,800 to 3,300 units a year**. It gets about **₹70,000 in subsidy** (₹60,000 PM Surya Ghar + ₹10,000 Tamil Nadu top-up). It suits small homes with a monthly bill of about **₹1,000 to ₹1,800**, but for many families **3kW turns out to be the smarter buy**. Here is why.

---

## 2kW Solar Units at a Glance

| Period | Units from 2kW (Tamil Nadu) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Per day (average)** | **8 - 10 units** |
| Per day (clear summer day) | 10 - 11 units |
| Per day (cloudy monsoon day) | 5 - 7.5 units |
| **Per month (average)** | **240 - 300 units** |
| Per TNEB bill (2 months) | 480 - 600 units |
| **Per year** | **2,800 - 3,300 units** |

**The math:** `2 kW × 5.2 peak sun hours × 0.78 performance ratio ≈ 8.1 units` on an average day, and close to 11 on bright summer days. Coastal Cuddalore homes sit in the middle of the range; drier [Panruti](/service-areas/panruti) and [Neyveli](/service-areas/neyveli) do slightly better.

## What Can 2kW Run?

| Load | Approx. units per day |
| :--- | :--- |
| 6-8 LED lights + 4 fans | 2 - 2.5 |
| Refrigerator | 1 - 1.3 |
| TV, Wi-Fi, laptop, chargers | 0.8 - 1.2 |
| Washing machine, mixer, iron | 0.6 - 1 |
| Water pump (0.5-1 HP, short run) | 0.4 - 0.7 |
| 1 ton inverter AC, about 3-4 hours | 2.5 - 3.5 |
| **Total** | **about 8 - 10 units** |

So 2kW covers a **2BHK home with light AC use**. If your AC runs most afternoons and nights, the [3kW system](/systems/3kw-solar-system-with-subsidy) is the right size. See [can a 1.5 ton AC run on solar](/blog/2026-04-25-can-1-5-ton-ac-run-on-solar-power-tamil-nadu).

## 2kW vs 3kW: The Subsidy Math Most People Miss

| | 2kW | 3kW |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Units per day | 8 - 10 | 12 - 15 |
| Market price (approx.) | ₹1.3 - 1.45 lakh | ₹1.8 - 2.0 lakh |
| Total subsidy (TN) | ₹70,000 | **₹1,00,000** |
| Real cost after subsidy | ₹60,000 - ₹75,000 | ₹80,000 - ₹1,00,000 |
| Cost per daily unit (after subsidy) | about ₹7,500 | about ₹6,700 |

For roughly **₹20,000-25,000 more** (after subsidy), 3kW gives you **about 50% more units** and covers an AC. Unless your roof is small or your usage is genuinely low, **3kW gives more value per rupee**.

## Check the 200 Free Units First

Since May 2026, Tamil Nadu homes using **500 units or less in a two-month bill get the first 200 units free**. If your bimonthly use is around 300-500 units, part of your consumption is already free, and solar saves you mainly the ₹4.50-6.00 units above 200. That still pays back, but the payback is slower than for high-bill homes.

Two good rules of thumb:
*   **Bimonthly use under 300 units:** solar savings are small; consider 1kW-2kW only if you expect usage to grow (new AC, more family members).
*   **Bimonthly use 500+ units:** you are paying the expensive slabs, and 3kW or more pays back fastest.

See how slabs work in our [TNEB slab rates guide](/blog/2026-04-30-how-to-calculate-eb-bill-tamil-nadu-examples).

## Roof Space and Design

*   **Panels:** four panels of 540-580 watts (older systems used six 330-watt panels).
*   **Roof area:** about **150-200 sq ft** of shade-free terrace.
*   **Structure:** a raised hot-dip galvanized structure for coastal homes; it keeps panels cooler and the terrace usable. See [structure types](/blog/2025-12-02-rooftop-solar-structure-types-tamil-nadu).
*   **Inverter:** a 2kW or 3kW on-grid inverter. Choosing a 3kW inverter lets you add panels later.

**Tip:** if you choose 2kW now because of budget, ask for a **3kW inverter and space reserved on the structure**. Adding one or two panels later is cheap, though the subsidy applies only to the capacity in your original application.

## Is 2kW Enough for Your Home? Quick Checklist

2kW is likely right if **most** of these are true:

*   Your bimonthly TNEB bill is about **400-600 units** in summer.
*   You have **no AC**, or one AC used only a few hours in the evening.
*   Your shade-free roof is **under about 250 sq ft**.
*   You do not plan a new AC, EV or big appliance in the next 2-3 years.

If two or more are false, look at **3kW**: it gives about 50% more units and ₹30,000 more subsidy for a small extra cost. A free site visit settles it with your real bills and roof measurements.

## How to Check a 2kW System Is Working Well

Open your inverter app on a clear day. A healthy 2kW system should show about **9-11 units** by evening in summer and **8-9** on normal sunny days. Consistently lower numbers mean dust, new shade or a wiring issue; our [maintenance guide](/blog/2026-01-10-solar-maintenance-guide-cuddalore) explains what to check.
