**Short answer:** a **5kW solar system produces about 20 to 25 units a day** in Tamil Nadu. That is **600 to 750 units a month** and roughly **7,200 to 8,500 units a year**. It comfortably runs a home with **two ACs**, and it qualifies for the full **₹1 lakh subsidy**.

---

## 5kW Solar Units at a Glance

| Period | Units from 5kW (Tamil Nadu) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Per day (average)** | **20 - 25 units** |
| Per day (clear summer day) | 25 - 27 units |
| Per day (cloudy monsoon day) | 15 - 19 units |
| **Per month (average)** | **600 - 750 units** |
| Per TNEB bill (2 months) | 1,200 - 1,500 units |
| **Per year** | **7,200 - 8,500 units** |

**The math:** `5 kW × 5.2 peak sun hours × 0.78 performance ratio ≈ 20.3 units` on an average day, rising past 25 in clear summer weather. A clean, shade-free, well-wired system on an elevated structure stays at the top of the range.

## Month by Month (Tamil Nadu Seasons)

| Season | Months | Units per day | Units per month |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Summer | March - May | 25 - 27 | 750 - 820 |
| Southwest monsoon | June - September | 21 - 24 | 640 - 720 |
| **Northeast monsoon** | **October - December** | **16 - 19** | **480 - 570** |
| Winter | January - February | 21 - 23 | 600 - 650 |

Summer, when your AC bill is highest, is exactly when 5kW produces the most. Extra units go to the grid through your net meter and are adjusted against your bill.

## What a 5kW System Can Run

| Load | Approx. units per day |
| :--- | :--- |
| Two 1.5 ton inverter ACs (about 8 hours each) | 12 - 16 |
| Refrigerator (double door) | 1.2 - 1.8 |
| Lights, fans, TV, Wi-Fi | 2 - 3 |
| Washing machine, mixer, iron | 1 - 2 |
| Water pump (1 HP, 30-45 minutes) | 0.5 - 0.8 |
| **Total** | **about 17 - 24 units** |

This is why 5kW is the usual choice for **3BHK and 4BHK homes, two-AC families, joint families and homes planning an EV**. If you have only one AC, [3kW is usually enough](/systems/3kw-solar-system-with-subsidy).

## Roof Space and Panels

*   **Panels:** about nine panels of 550-580 watts (older designs used 15-16 panels of 330 watts).
*   **Shade-free roof area:** about **350 to 500 square feet**.
*   **Structure:** on Cuddalore's coast we use a raised **hot-dip galvanized** structure that keeps panels cooler (more units) and lets you use the terrace below. See [best solar structure for coastal Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-rooftop-solar-structure-types-tamil-nadu).
*   **Connection:** a 5kW system usually works on single phase, but if your house load is high TNEB may suggest three phase. Read [single phase to 3-phase upgrade](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).

### Savings
At 650 units a month, a 5kW system can cut a high summer bill of **₹5,000 - ₹7,000** down to a few hundred rupees, depending on your slab. Typical payback after the ₹1 lakh subsidy is around **3 to 4 years**, and the panels keep producing for 25 years.
