## Red Flags to Walk Away From

*   **"Subsidy guaranteed, no documents needed."** Every application needs Aadhaar, EB bill and bank details in matching names.
*   **Price far below ₹1.6 lakh for 3kW with DCR panels.** Something is being cut: panels, structure or earthing.
*   **Plain GI or painted structure near the beach.** It will rust; ask for hot-dip galvanized.
*   **No written warranty or only verbal promises.**
*   **Company not listed on the PM Surya Ghar portal.**
*   **Advance of 100% before any material arrives.**

More warning signs: [7 solar scam warnings](/blog/2026-01-09-solar-scam-warnings-cuddalore).

## Coastal Puducherry: Structure and Maintenance Tips

*   **Hot-dip galvanized structure** with stainless steel nuts and bolts; avoid mixed metals that corrode.
*   **Rinse panels with plain water** every 2-4 weeks near the beach; salt film cuts output.
*   **Inverter in a covered, ventilated spot**, not facing the sea breeze directly.
*   **Pre-monsoon check** before October: bolts, earthing and cables. See [solar panels in rain and cyclones](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-panels-rain-cyclone-cuddalore-monsoon-guide).
