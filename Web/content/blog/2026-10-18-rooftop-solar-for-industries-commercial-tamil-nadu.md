**Short answer:** rooftop solar is one of the best investments for **factories, warehouses, hospitals, schools, offices and malls** in Tamil Nadu, because they use most of their power **in the daytime**, exactly when solar produces. In 2026, systems of **20 kW and above** typically cost about **₹40,000-55,000 per kW** installed. Businesses get **no PM Surya Ghar subsidy**, but can claim **40% accelerated depreciation**, and payback is often **3-5 years**. You can **buy the plant (CAPEX)** or **pay per unit with no upfront cost (OPEX)**.

> *Market averages as of October 2026, not quotes. Tax and metering rules depend on your tariff and business structure; confirm with your auditor and the current TNERC order.*

## CAPEX vs OPEX: Buy or Pay Per Unit?

| | CAPEX (you own it) | OPEX / PPA (developer owns it) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Upfront cost | Full system cost | None |
| Savings | All bill savings are yours | You pay per unit, below grid rate |
| Tax benefit | 40% accelerated depreciation to you | Goes to the developer |
| Maintenance | Your responsibility (or AMC) | Developer's responsibility |
| Contract | None after purchase | Long-term agreement, often 15-25 years |
| Best for | Profitable businesses with cash or loans | Businesses that want zero investment |

Most small and mid-size businesses in Cuddalore district choose **CAPEX** because payback is short and the tax benefit is valuable. Large plants sometimes prefer OPEX to keep capital for production.

## Tax Benefit: 40% Accelerated Depreciation

A solar plant owned by a tax-paying business can be depreciated at **40% per year** on the written-down value. On a ₹20 lakh plant, the first-year depreciation is ₹8 lakh, which reduces taxable profit. The full worked example is in [accelerated depreciation for solar](/blog/2026-01-19-commercial-solar-tax-benefits). Always confirm with your chartered accountant.

## Roof Types: RCC and Metal Sheet

| Roof | Mounting | Watch for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| RCC terrace | Galvanized structure, often raised | Waterproofing at anchor points |
| Metal sheet (trapezoidal) | Rails clamped to seams or purlins | Sheet age, rust, load capacity |
| Asbestos sheet | Separate frame, no drilling into sheets | Safety; often needs a sub-structure |
| Ground (open land) | Ground-mounted structure | Land use, fencing, cleaning access |

Coastal industrial areas such as SIPCOT Cuddalore need **hot-dip galvanized** structures and **stainless fasteners** because of salt air: [rust-proof solar for coastal sites](/blog/2026-01-05-coastal-solar-rust-proof-panels-cuddalore).

## Metering and TNEB Rules for Businesses

*   Commercial and industrial rooftop systems connect through TNPDCL with a meter arrangement set by **TNERC** for your tariff category.
*   Charges and settlement rules for non-domestic solar have been **revised and debated** over the years, so check the **current TNERC order** before investing.
*   Larger systems need a **three-phase** connection and suitable **sanctioned load or contract demand**.

More: [Tamil Nadu solar net metering policy](/blog/2026-10-18-tamil-nadu-solar-net-metering-policy).

## Payback: What Drives It

1.  **Daytime self-consumption.** The more solar units you use directly during working hours, the faster the payback.
2.  **Your tariff.** Higher commercial and industrial tariffs make each solar unit more valuable.
3.  **Working days.** A 6-day factory uses more solar than a 5-day office.
4.  **Tax benefit.** Depreciation shortens payback for profitable businesses.
5.  **Maintenance.** Regular cleaning keeps output high, especially near dust and industry: [solar maintenance guide](/blog/2026-01-10-solar-maintenance-guide-cuddalore).

Typical result: **about 3-5 years** for businesses with strong daytime use.

## Project Steps for a Business

1.  Share **12 months of EB bills** and load details.
2.  **Site survey:** roof strength, shade, space, cable routes, transformer.
3.  **Design and proposal:** size, layout, CAPEX or OPEX options, payback.
4.  **TNPDCL approval** and any load or meter changes.
5.  **Installation** (days to a few weeks, depending on size).
6.  **Inspection, metering and commissioning.**
7.  **Monitoring and AMC** to keep generation high.

## Hybrid or Battery for Critical Loads?

Most businesses choose **on-grid** for the best return. For critical loads (billing systems, CCTV, lab equipment, cold storage controls), a **small UPS or a hybrid inverter with batteries** for only those loads costs far less than backing up the whole plant. See [solar with battery backup price](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-with-battery-backup-price-tamil-nadu).
