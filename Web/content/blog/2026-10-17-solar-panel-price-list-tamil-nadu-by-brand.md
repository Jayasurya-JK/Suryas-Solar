**Short answer:** in 2026, **DCR solar panels from major Indian brands cost about ₹22-29 per watt** in Tamil Nadu, so the panels for a **3kW system cost roughly ₹66,000-87,000**. A **complete 3kW on-grid system costs about ₹1.8-2.1 lakh installed**, and after the **₹1 lakh subsidy** (₹78,000 + ₹22,000 TN top-up for eligible homes) you pay about **₹80,000 to ₹1,10,000**. Brand differences are small; DCR status, wattage and installation quality matter more.

> *All prices are market averages as of October 2026, not our quotes. Prices move every few months with cell costs and stock. We give a written quote after a free site visit.*

## What Makes Up the System Price

| Component | Share of 3kW price | What to check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Solar panels | about 45 - 55% | DCR certificate, model number, wattage |
| On-grid inverter | about 12 - 18% | BIS registration, warranty, local service |
| Mounting structure | about 10 - 15% | Hot-dip galvanized for coastal areas, height |
| Cables, DCDB/ACDB, earthing, lightning arrester | about 8 - 12% | Copper DC cable, 3 separate earth pits |
| Installation, net meter, paperwork | about 5 - 10% | Who handles TNEB and subsidy follow-up |

This is why two quotes with "the same panels" can differ by ₹30,000 or more. A cheap quote usually saves on the **structure, cables or earthing**, which are exactly the parts that fail in [Cuddalore](/service-areas/cuddalore)'s salty, windy climate. ## 5 Ways to Get a Fair Price (Without Losing Quality)

1.  **Compare per watt, not per panel.** A 540 W panel at ₹14,000 is cheaper per watt than a 440 W panel at ₹12,000.
2.  **Insist on DCR for subsidy homes.** Saving ₹15,000 on non-DCR panels can cost you the entire ₹1 lakh subsidy.
3.  **Ask for an itemised quote** with brands and models of panels, inverter, structure and cables.
4.  **Do not overpay for small efficiency gains.** A 0.5% efficiency difference is about 2% more units.
5.  **Book before the deadline rush.** Prices and waiting times rise near scheme deadlines. See [PM Surya Ghar last date 2027](/blog/2026-10-17-pm-surya-ghar-last-date-2027-scheme-ending).
