**Short answer:** **yes, Tamil Nadu has net metering for rooftop solar.** TNPDCL (TNEB) replaces your meter with a **bi-directional net meter** that records units you **import** from the grid and units your system **exports**. You pay mainly for the **difference**, so daytime solar export covers night-time use. Your system size generally **cannot exceed your sanctioned load**, and how leftover export units are settled is set by **TNERC** rules. Homes that net-meter also get the **up to ₹1 lakh subsidy** if eligible.

> *Rules summarised as of October 2026. Net metering terms are set by the Tamil Nadu Electricity Regulatory Commission (TNERC) and can change; check the current order or ask us before you install.*

## Net Metering in Tamil Nadu at a Glance

| Point | How it works for homes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Who installs the meter | TNPDCL (TNEB) section office |
| Meter type | Bi-directional net meter |
| What is billed | Import minus export (net units), plus fixed charges |
| Capacity limit | Generally up to your sanctioned load |
| Approval | Feasibility check, then inspection after installation |
| Typical time | 2 - 6 weeks after installation |
| Rules set by | TNERC (Tamil Nadu Electricity Regulatory Commission) |
| Subsidy | PM Surya Ghar + TN top-up for eligible domestic homes |

## How Net Metering Works Day to Day

| Time | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| Morning to afternoon | Solar powers your home; extra units **export** to the grid |
| Evening and night | Your home **imports** from the grid |
| Billing cycle (two months) | TNPDCL reads import and export; you pay for net units |

**Example:** in two months your home uses 700 units. Your 3kW system makes 800 units: 350 used directly in the day, 450 exported. At night you import 350. Net billed units: 350 import minus 450 export = **0** (plus fixed charges). The extra export is credited as per current rules.

## Capacity Rules: How Big Can Your System Be?

*   Solar capacity is generally limited to your **sanctioned load** (shown on your EB card or bill).
*   Want a bigger system? Apply for a **load increase** before or with the solar application.
*   Large systems often need a **three-phase** connection. See [single phase to 3-phase upgrade](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).
*   For the subsidy, the central amount is capped at the 3kW level (₹78,000), so size to your bill, not the maximum allowed.

---

## Leftover Units: What Happens to Extra Export?

If your system exports more than you import over a period, the surplus is handled under the TNERC settlement rule in force. These rules have been revised over the years, so we do not quote a fixed rate here. What matters for planning:
*   **Size your system to your use**, not far beyond it. Big surpluses add little value.
*   **Check the export and net units** on each bill.
*   Ask your installer to explain the **current settlement** before signing.

## The Net Metering Process in Tamil Nadu

1.  **Apply** on the PM Surya Ghar portal or TNPDCL's Unified Solar Rooftop Portal (USRP) with your consumer number. See [TNEB solar application: how to apply and check status](/blog/2026-10-17-tneb-solar-application-apply-check-status).
2.  **Feasibility approval** by the section office (load, dues, transformer capacity).
3.  **Installation** by a registered vendor.
4.  **Work completion upload** and **net meter agreement**.
5.  **Inspection** and **net meter fixing** by TNPDCL.
6.  **Commissioning**, then the subsidy is credited.

Detailed local steps: [how to get a TNEB net meter in Cuddalore](/blog/2026-01-04-how-to-get-tneb-net-meter-cuddalore-2026). Waiting for the meter? A [zero export device](/blog/2026-01-18-zero-export-device-guide) lets you use solar for your own load meanwhile.

## Net Metering vs Other Options

| Option | What it means | Who it suits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Net metering** | Import and export netted on one meter | Most homes |
| **Gross metering** | All solar sold to the grid at a fixed rate, all use bought | Rare for homes |
| **Group / virtual net metering** | One system's units credited to several connections | Some apartment and institutional cases; check current TNERC provisions |
| **Zero export** | No export; solar used only inside the building | Before net meter, or where export is not allowed |

Apartment associations can use common-area solar with net metering on the common connection: [solar for apartments: RWA subsidy guide](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-for-apartments-tamil-nadu-rwa-subsidy).

## Commercial Connections

Shops, offices and factories can also install rooftop solar, but the metering arrangement and charges for non-domestic consumers follow separate TNERC provisions, which have been revised and debated. Check the current order for your tariff before investing; we work it out from your bills. See [solar for shops and small businesses](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-for-shops-small-business-cuddalore).

## Before You Sign: Net Metering Checklist

1.  Check your **sanctioned load** and plan any load increase.
2.  Clear any **pending EB dues** on the connection.
3.  Make sure the **EB bill name matches your Aadhaar** and bank account.
4.  Ask your installer to explain the **current settlement rule** for surplus units.
