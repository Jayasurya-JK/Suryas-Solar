**Short answer:** most PM Surya Ghar applications are rejected or stuck for **simple, fixable reasons**: the **name on the EB bill does not match Aadhaar or the bank account**, the **sanctioned load is lower than the system size**, there are **pending EB dues**, the connection is **not domestic**, or the system was installed by an **unregistered vendor** or with **non-DCR panels**. Find the reason on the portal, fix it, and re-apply quickly, because the Tamil Nadu top-up is limited.

> *Based on the scheme rules and the cases we see in Cuddalore district, as of October 2026. The exact remark on your application is the best guide.*

## The 8 Most Common Rejection Reasons

| # | Reason | Stage where it appears | Fix | Time to fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Name mismatch (EB bill vs Aadhaar vs bank) | Registration / subsidy | Name transfer or correction, matching bank account | 1 - 4 weeks |
| 2 | Sanctioned load lower than system size | TNEB feasibility | Apply for load increase | 1 - 3 weeks |
| 3 | Pending EB dues | TNEB feasibility | Clear dues, get receipt | 1 - 3 days |
| 4 | Non-domestic connection | Registration | Apply on a domestic connection only | - |
| 5 | Already received a rooftop subsidy | Registration | Only extra capacity may qualify; ask us | - |

| 7 | Unregistered vendor | Inspection / subsidy | Cannot be fixed later; use a registered vendor | - |
| 8 | Wrong or missing documents and photos | Any stage | Re-upload clear documents | 1 - 3 days |

## 1. Name Mismatch (The Number One Problem)

The applicant's name must match on the **EB connection, Aadhaar and bank account**. Common cases in Tamil Nadu:
*   The meter is still in a **late parent's name**.
*   The bill says "K. Murugan" but Aadhaar says "Murugan Kannan".
*   The bank account is a joint account in the spouse's name.

**Fix:** start the **EB name transfer** at your TNEB section office (needs ownership proof and, for inheritance, legal heir documents), or apply in the name of the person on the bill if they own the house. Make sure the **bank account** is in the same name.

---

## 2. Sanctioned Load Lower Than System Size

If your sanctioned load is 2 kW and you apply for a 3 kW system, TNEB may reject feasibility or ask for a **load increase**. Check the load printed on your EB card or bill.

**Fix:** apply for load enhancement (small fee and deposit), then re-apply or resubmit. Larger systems may also need [single phase to 3-phase upgrade](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).

---

## 3. Pending EB Dues

Any unpaid amount on the connection can block feasibility. **Fix:** pay, keep the receipt, and resubmit.

---

## 4. Non-Domestic Connection

PM Surya Ghar is for **residential (domestic) connections**. Shops, offices and agricultural connections are rejected. Apartments can apply through the RWA for common areas: see [solar for apartments: RWA subsidy guide](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-for-apartments-tamil-nadu-rwa-subsidy). Businesses have other benefits: [solar for shops and small businesses](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-for-shops-small-business-cuddalore).

---

## 5. Already Received a Subsidy

The subsidy is given **once per connection** for rooftop solar. If an older system on your house got a subsidy, a new application may be rejected or limited to the remaining capacity. Bring your old documents so we can check.

---

## 6 and 7. Wrong Panels or Unregistered Installer

These are the most painful because they appear **after** you have paid:
*   Panels must be **DCR** (Indian-made cells) and on the approved list. Non-DCR panels lose the entire subsidy.
*   The installer must be a **registered vendor** on the portal.

**Prevention:** get the **panel model, DCR certificate and vendor registration** in writing before paying any advance. See [how to compare solar quotations](/blog/2026-10-17-how-to-compare-solar-quotations-checklist) and [7 solar scam warnings](/blog/2026-01-09-solar-scam-warnings-cuddalore).

---

## 8. Documents and Photos

Blurry Aadhaar scans, a cropped EB bill, or site photos that do not show the full system can cause a query or rejection. **Fix:** re-upload clear, complete copies.

## Status Meanings (Quick Guide)

| Portal status | What it usually means | Your action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Application submitted | Waiting for TNEB feasibility | Wait 1-2 weeks; check load and dues |
| Feasibility approved | You can install | Book installation with your vendor |
| Feasibility rejected | Load, dues or connection issue | Read the remark, fix, resubmit |
| Installation details submitted | Waiting for inspection | Wait for TNEB visit and net meter |
| Commissioned | System approved | Subsidy usually in about 30 days |
| Subsidy pending / rejected | Bank or document issue | Check bank name and uploads, raise grievance |

## How to Escalate a Stuck Application

1.  **Check the remark** on the portal and note your application number.
2.  **Visit or call your TNEB section office** with the application number.
3.  **Raise a grievance** on the PM Surya Ghar portal if there is no movement.
4.  Keep **screenshots and receipts** of every step.
