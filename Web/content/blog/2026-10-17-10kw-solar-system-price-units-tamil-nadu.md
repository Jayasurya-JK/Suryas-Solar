**Short answer:** a **10kW solar system produces about 40 to 50 units a day** in Tamil Nadu (**1,200-1,500 units a month**) and costs about **₹5 to 6.5 lakh** installed in 2026. It suits **large homes with 3-4 ACs or an EV**, **joint families**, and **small businesses** like clinics, shops, offices and schools. Homes get the same capped subsidy as 3kW (**up to ₹1 lakh**), while businesses get **no PM Surya Ghar subsidy** but can claim **tax depreciation** benefits.

| Units per day | **40 - 50** |

| Inverter | Usually one 10kW three-phase on-grid inverter |

| Typical installed price | **₹5 - 6.5 lakh** (market range) |

**The math:** `10 kW × 5.2 peak sun hours × 0.78 ≈ 40.5 units` on an average day, rising to 50+ on clear summer days.

| Three-phase on-grid inverter | about 12 - 18% |

| DC/AC cables, protection boxes, earthing, lightning arrester | about 8 - 12% |
| Installation, TNEB process, net meter | about 5 - 10% |

**Why the price range is wide (₹5-6.5 lakh):**
*   **DCR vs non-DCR panels:** subsidy homes must use DCR panels, which cost more. A business that does not need subsidy can use non-DCR panels and pay less.
*   **TOPCon vs Mono PERC:** TOPCon gives more units in heat but costs a little more.
*   **Structure height:** raised structures (so the terrace stays usable) need more steel.
*   **Inverter brand:** premium European brands cost more than good Chinese or Indian brands. See [best on-grid solar inverters](/blog/2026-10-17-best-on-grid-solar-inverter-india).

## Subsidy: Homes vs Businesses

| | Residential home | Business / commercial |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PM Surya Ghar central subsidy | Up to ₹78,000 (capped at 3kW) | Not eligible |
| Tamil Nadu top-up (G.O. 102) | Up to ₹22,000 (eligible homes) | Not eligible |
| Tax benefit | None | **Accelerated depreciation** on solar assets (ask your auditor) |
| Panels required | DCR | Any ALMM-listed panel |

For a **home**, the ₹1 lakh subsidy makes a 10kW system cost about **₹4-5.5 lakh** net. For a **business**, the depreciation benefit can reduce tax significantly in the first years. Read [commercial solar tax benefits](/blog/2026-01-19-commercial-solar-tax-benefits) and [₹1 lakh solar subsidy in Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-tamil-nadu-puducherry).

## Savings and Payback

A home or business using **2,400+ units per two months** pays the **₹10-11 per unit** top slabs on much of it. A 10kW system can cut a two-month bill of **₹20,000-28,000** down to a small fraction.

| Example | Two-month bill without solar | Approx. yearly saving | Net cost | Payback |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Large home, 2,400 units / two months | about ₹22,000 | about ₹1.2 - 1.3 lakh | ₹4 - 5.5 lakh (after ₹1 lakh subsidy) | **about 3 - 4.5 years** |
| Small business (commercial tariff) | varies by tariff | depends on tariff and daytime use | ₹5 - 6.5 lakh (before tax benefit) | **often 4 - 6 years** |

These are estimates using approximate domestic slab rates; commercial tariffs differ. See the full method in [solar payback after ₹1 lakh subsidy](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-payback-after-1-lakh-subsidy-tamil-nadu).

## Technical Points Before You Install 10kW

1.  **Three-phase connection.** Most 10kW systems use a three-phase inverter. If you are on single phase, TNEB will ask for an upgrade first. See [single phase to 3-phase upgrade](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).
2.  **Sanctioned load.** Your sanctioned load must support the system size; a load increase may be needed.
3.  **Roof strength and layout.** 17-19 panels and a raised structure add weight and wind load; the roof must be checked, and the structure must be designed for coastal wind.
4.  **Shading.** Larger arrays are more likely to have part-shading from water tanks or nearby buildings. Plan string layout carefully: [shadow analysis guide](/blog/2026-01-12-solar-shadow-analysis-guide-cuddalore).
5.  **Earthing and protection.** Bigger systems need properly sized surge protection and three separate earthing pits: [earthing guide](/blog/2026-01-13-solar-earthing-safety-guide).
6.  **Monitoring.** Use the inverter's app to track daily units; a 10kW system should show 40+ units on a sunny day.

## 10kW On-Grid or Hybrid?

For most large homes and businesses, **on-grid** gives the best return. A **hybrid** 10kW system with batteries makes sense only if power cuts disrupt critical work (clinics, server rooms). Batteries for that size are expensive, so many businesses choose on-grid solar plus a small UPS for critical loads. Compare: [on-grid vs hybrid solar](/blog/2026-01-06-on-grid-vs-hybrid-solar-tamil-nadu).
