**Short answer:** **Premier and Adani are both strong choices.** Their latest TOPCon panels are within about half a percent of each other in efficiency (roughly **22-23%**). **Adani** generally has **wider DCR (subsidy-eligible) availability** and clearly published warranty terms. **Premier** can be **slightly cheaper** for a comparable DCR model. For a Tamil Nadu home on the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, the right choice is the **DCR model your installer can actually supply, with a full datasheet and warranty card**, installed properly.

The panel brand is about 20% of a good solar system. The other 80% is structure, wiring, earthing and the installer.

## 1. Efficiency: Practically a Tie

The difference between a 22.3% and a 22.8% panel is about **2% more units from the same roof area**. On a 3kW system that is roughly 0.3 units a day. It matters only if your roof is very small. If roof space is not a problem, do not pay a big premium for half a percent.

What matters more in Tamil Nadu is **heat**. Panels lose output as they get hotter. **TOPCon panels from both brands handle heat better than older Mono PERC**, which is why we recommend TOPCon for our hot, humid coast. See also [bifacial vs monocrystalline panels](/blog/2026-01-07-mono-vs-bifacial-solar-panels).

## 2. DCR Status: The Deciding Factor for Subsidy Homes

To get the **₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy** (and the **₹22,000 Tamil Nadu top-up**), your panels must be **DCR**: made with Indian cells and listed under the government's approved lists.

*   DCR panels cost more than non-DCR panels. One April 2026 price survey reported about **₹22 per watt for DCR TOPCon vs about ₹14 per watt for non-DCR TOPCon**.
*   Both Premier and Adani make DCR models, but **not every model is DCR**. The exact model number must match the approved list.
*   Ask your installer for: the **model number**, **DCR certificate**, and the **datasheet**, and check the serial numbers on the invoice.

If an installer offers "Adani" or "Premier" at a price far below the market, it is often a **non-DCR model**, which will make you **lose the whole subsidy**. Read our [solar scam warnings](/blog/2026-01-09-solar-scam-warnings-cuddalore).

## 3. Warranty and Service

Both brands offer a **25-30 year performance warranty** (the panel will still give a guaranteed percentage of its rated power) and a separate **product warranty** for manufacturing defects. Compare:

*   **Product warranty years** (10 vs 12 years makes a difference).
*   **Year-25 or year-30 guaranteed output** (for example 87% or more).
*   **Who handles your claim locally.** A warranty is only useful if your installer helps you claim it. This is a big reason to buy from a local installer with an office near you.

---

## 4. Price

Module-only prices for both brands are close, roughly **₹22-29 per watt** in 2026 depending on DCR status and wattage. On a 3kW system, even a ₹2 per watt difference is only about **₹6,000**, which is small compared with the ₹1 lakh subsidy and 25 years of savings.

Typical installed price for a 3kW DCR system in Tamil Nadu is about **₹1.8 - 2.0 lakh before subsidy** (market average). See [solar panel cost in Tamil Nadu 2026](/blog/2026-05-04-solar-panel-cost-tamil-nadu-2026-price-subsidy-savings).
