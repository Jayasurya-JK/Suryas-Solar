**Short answer:** to apply for rooftop solar with TNEB (now TNPDCL) in 2026, register on the **national PM Surya Ghar portal** (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) or **TNPDCL's Unified Solar Rooftop Portal (USRP)** at tnebltd.gov.in/usrp, using your **EB consumer number** and the **mobile number linked to it**. Choose a **registered vendor**, wait for **feasibility approval (1-2 weeks)**, install, and the **net meter and subsidy** follow. To **check your status**, log in with the same mobile and consumer number and open your application.

> *Portal names, steps and timelines as of October 2026. Screens change from time to time; your installer can check the status with you.*

---

## Which Portal Should You Use?

| Portal | Address | Use it for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PM Surya Ghar (national)** | pmsuryaghar.gov.in | Registering for the central subsidy, choosing a vendor, tracking subsidy |
| **TNPDCL Unified Solar Rooftop Portal (USRP)** | tnebltd.gov.in/usrp | Tamil Nadu rooftop applications with or without subsidy, net metering |

Both lead to the same TNPDCL approval process for your connection. Domestic homes that want the subsidy apply under the subsidy option; shops and other categories apply without subsidy. **Applications registered on or after 5 August 2026** are eligible for the **₹22,000 Tamil Nadu top-up** in addition to the ₹78,000 central subsidy. Details: [₹1 lakh solar subsidy in Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-tamil-nadu-puducherry).

## Step by Step: How to Apply

1.  **Keep your EB bill and phone ready.** You need the consumer (service connection) number and the mobile number registered with TNPDCL. OTPs go to this number.
2.  **Register on the portal.** Select Tamil Nadu, TNPDCL, and enter your consumer number. Check that the name shown matches your Aadhaar.
3.  **Fill the application.** Choose rooftop solar with subsidy (for homes), enter the system size (for example 3kW) and upload the requested documents.
4.  **Choose a registered vendor.** Only vendors listed on the portal can install systems that receive the subsidy.
5.  **Wait for feasibility approval.** The section office checks your sanctioned load, dues and local transformer capacity, usually in 1-2 weeks.
6.  **Installation.** Your vendor installs the system (1-3 days) and uploads the details, panel serial numbers and photos.
7.  **Inspection and net meter.** TNPDCL inspects and fixes a bi-directional net meter, usually in 2-6 weeks.
8.  **Commissioning and subsidy.** After commissioning is recorded, the central subsidy is usually credited in about 30 days; the TN top-up is paid separately by TEDA.

Not sure you qualify? Use the 30-second [subsidy eligibility checker](/subsidy-eligibility) first.

## Documents You Need

| Document | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Latest EB bill | Must be in the applicant's name |
| Aadhaar card | Mobile number linked for OTP |
| Property proof | Tax receipt or sale deed |
| Bank passbook or cancelled cheque | Same name as the applicant |
| Roof photo | Clear view of the space for panels |
| Technical documents | Uploaded by your installer after installation |

**The most common delay** is a name mismatch between the EB bill and Aadhaar. If the meter is still in a parent's name, start the name transfer first.

## How to Check Your Application Status

1.  Open the portal where you applied (PM Surya Ghar or USRP).
2.  Log in with your **registered mobile number** and **consumer number** (OTP based).
3.  Open **your application** to see the current stage and any **remarks**.

### What Each Status Usually Means

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Application submitted | Waiting for feasibility check | Wait 1-2 weeks; clear any pending dues |
| Feasibility approved | You can install | Book installation with your registered vendor |
| Feasibility rejected | Load, dues or connection issue | Read the remark, fix it, resubmit |
| Installation details submitted | Waiting for inspection | Inspection and net meter usually in 2-6 weeks |
| Commissioned | System approved | Central subsidy usually in about 30 days |
| Subsidy pending | Bank or document issue | Check bank details and uploads, then raise a query |

If you see a rejection, read [PM Surya Ghar application rejected: 8 reasons and fixes](/blog/2026-10-17-pm-surya-ghar-application-rejected-reasons-fixes).

## Why Applications Get Stuck (and Fixes)

| Problem | Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sanctioned load lower than system size | Apply for load enhancement with your application |
| Pending EB dues | Pay and keep the receipt |
| Name mismatch (bill, Aadhaar, bank) | Name transfer or matching bank account |
| Wrong mobile number on the connection | Update it at the section office so OTPs reach you |
| Non-DCR panels or unregistered vendor | Avoid this from the start; it cannot be fixed later |
