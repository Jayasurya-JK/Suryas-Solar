## 8 Features That Actually Matter

1.  **BIS certification.** Grid-connected inverters sold in India must carry BIS registration (the relevant Indian standards cover safety and grid connection). Ask for the certificate of the exact model.
2.  **Dual MPPT.** Two independent inputs let panels on different roof faces, or with some shade, perform better.
3.  **Wide AC voltage range.** In many Tamil Nadu areas grid voltage swings high at noon. An inverter with a suitable, correctly set voltage window trips less often. See [voltage problems and hybrid solar](/blog/2026-01-15-voltage-fluctuation-hybrid-solar).
4.  **IP65 rating.** Protection against dust and rain if mounted outdoors; even better, mount it in a shaded, covered spot.
5.  **Efficiency above 97%.** Every good brand meets this; do not pay extra for 0.2%.
6.  **App and Wi-Fi monitoring.** Lets you see daily units and spot problems early.
7.  **Warranty.** 5 years minimum; 7-10 years (or extendable) is better.
8.  **Local service.** Who replaces it in Cuddalore within days, not weeks? This matters more than anything above.

| Solar system | Typical inverter | Connection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

Matching inverter size to panel capacity matters: a heavily undersized inverter "clips" the noon peak, and a heavily oversized one costs more for no gain. ## On-Grid vs Hybrid Inverter

| | On-grid inverter | Hybrid inverter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Battery | No | Yes |
| Works in power cut | No (anti-islanding safety) | Yes, for backed-up loads |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Best for | Lowest bill, fastest payback | Areas with frequent cuts |

An on-grid inverter **must shut down during a power cut** so it does not send power into lines that TNEB staff may be repairing. This is a safety feature, not a fault. If cuts are frequent, read [Exide solar battery review](/blog/2026-10-17-exide-solar-panel-battery-review-tamil-nadu) and [on-grid vs hybrid](/blog/2026-01-06-on-grid-vs-hybrid-solar-tamil-nadu).

## Micro-Inverters and Optimisers

For roofs with **heavy or changing shade**, **micro-inverters** (one per panel) or **optimisers** can recover lost units, at a higher cost. For a clear, shade-free terrace, a good string inverter is the better value. Details: [Enphase micro-inverter review](/blog/2026-01-16-enphase-micro-inverter-review).

## Where to Mount Your Inverter (Tamil Nadu Tips)

*   **Shade and airflow:** heat reduces inverter output and life. Mount it on a shaded wall or indoors near the distribution box, never in direct afternoon sun.
*   **Above flood level:** in coastal Cuddalore, keep it well above any water that can collect during heavy rain.
*   **Short AC cable:** close to your meter/DB to reduce losses.
*   **Wi-Fi reach:** so monitoring keeps working.
*   **Clearance:** leave space around it for cooling and service.

More monsoon safety: [solar panels in rain and cyclones](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-panels-rain-cyclone-cuddalore-monsoon-guide).

## Inverter Warning Signs

| Sign | Possible cause | What to do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Switches off at noon on sunny days | High grid voltage | Ask installer to check settings; raise a TNEB voltage complaint |
| Units suddenly drop | String fault, loose connector, shading | Check app, call installer |
| Fan noise or very hot body | Poor ventilation | Relocate to shade |
| Error codes on display/app | Earth fault, insulation fault | Do not reset repeatedly; call installer |
