## 1. Reliability: Who Has the Proof?

For a 25-year product, **independent testing** is the best evidence. Waaree's TOPCon modules were named a **Kiwa PVEL Top Performer** in 2026 for the fifth consecutive year; PVEL stress-tests panels for damp heat, thermal cycling and mechanical load, which is close to [Cuddalore](/service-areas/cuddalore)'s coastal reality. Adani and Tata are also large, established makers; ask for the test reports of the specific model you are offered.

---

## 2. Efficiency and Heat

All three offer **TOPCon** panels in roughly the **22-23%** range. TOPCon loses less output in heat than older Mono PERC, which matters on hot Tamil Nadu terraces. A 0.5% difference in efficiency is only about 2% more units, so do not overpay for it unless your roof is small. More: [bifacial vs monocrystalline](/blog/2026-01-07-mono-vs-bifacial-solar-panels).

---

## 3. Subsidy: DCR Availability

For the **₹1 lakh subsidy**, panels must be **DCR**. All three make DCR models. Adani and Tata have large DCR output; Tata's Tirunelveli plant produced most of its 2025 modules as DCR. Near the **March 2027 deadline**, availability may tighten, so book early: [PM Surya Ghar last date 2027](/blog/2026-10-17-pm-surya-ghar-last-date-2027-scheme-ending).

---

## 4. Price

Module prices are close (about **₹22-29 per watt**), and on a 3kW system a ₹2/W difference is only about **₹6,000**. Typical complete 3kW DCR systems cost about **₹1.8 - 2.1 lakh** before subsidy (market range), roughly **₹80,000 - ₹1,10,000** after the full ₹1 lakh. See [solar panel cost in Tamil Nadu 2026](/blog/2026-05-04-solar-panel-cost-tamil-nadu-2026-price-subsidy-savings).

## 5. Warranty and Service

Compare **product warranty years** (10 vs 12), **guaranteed output at year 25 or 30**, and **who handles claims locally**. A strong local installer who stays in business is your real warranty.

## Choosing by Budget and Roof

*   **Tight budget, normal roof:** pick whichever of the three has a **DCR Mono PERC or TOPCon** model in stock at the best price; all are reliable.
*   **Hot terrace, want maximum units:** choose **TOPCon** from any of them; it handles heat better.
*   **Raised structure over a white terrace:** consider **bifacial / glass-to-glass TOPCon** for extra rear-side units.
*   **Small roof, big bill:** take the **highest-wattage panel** available so you fit more kW.
*   **Want simplest after-sales:** a brand your installer is an **authorised partner** for, so warranty claims go through one local office.

For most 3kW Cuddalore homes, the honest answer is: **any of the three, DCR, TOPCon, properly installed.**

## How to Avoid Fake or Non-DCR Panels

1.  Get the **model number** and **DCR certificate** in writing.
2.  Match the **label on every panel** with the datasheet.
3.  Make sure **serial numbers** are on the invoice (needed for subsidy upload).
4.  Be wary of prices far below the market; non-DCR panels **lose you the entire subsidy**.
5.  Buy from a **registered PM Surya Ghar vendor** who will be around for warranty claims.
