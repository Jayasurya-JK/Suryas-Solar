**Short answer:** in 2026, Puducherry homes get the **central PM Surya Ghar subsidy of up to ₹78,000** for a 3kW or larger rooftop system (₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW). Puducherry has **also reported its own state top-up** for homes, with reports mentioning **about ₹9,000-10,000 per kW, up to about ₹30,000**; the amount and availability change, so confirm it with the **Electricity Department, Puducherry (PED)**. The **₹22,000 Tamil Nadu top-up does not apply** in Puducherry.

> *Central amounts are from the PM Surya Ghar scheme. The Puducherry state top-up figures come from published reports, not an official notice we could verify, as of October 2026. We confirm the current position before you apply.*

## Who Is Eligible?

*   **Domestic connection** from the Electricity Department, Puducherry, in the **house owner's name**.
*   **Your own roof** with shade-free space (about 80-100 sq ft per kW).
*   **Grid-connected system** with a net meter. Off-grid systems are not covered.
*   **DCR panels** (Indian-made cells) installed by a **vendor registered on the PM Surya Ghar portal**.
*   **No earlier rooftop solar subsidy** on the same connection.

Commercial and industrial connections do not get PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Apartments can apply through their association for common areas: see [solar for apartments: RWA subsidy guide](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-for-apartments-tamil-nadu-rwa-subsidy). ## How to Apply in Puducherry

1.  **Register on the PM Surya Ghar portal** (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) with your PED consumer number and the mobile number linked to your connection.
2.  **Apply for rooftop solar** and choose a registered vendor.
3.  **Feasibility check by PED.** The Electricity Department checks your connection and sanctioned load. The department also runs a Solar Rooftop Portal at electricity.py.gov.in for net metering applications.
4.  **Installation** by your vendor, usually 1-3 days.
5.  **Inspection and net meter** by PED under the JERC net metering regulations.
6.  **Subsidy credit** to your bank account after commissioning.

Keep your **EB bill, Aadhaar, property proof and bank details** ready, all in the same name. The full process usually takes **6-10 weeks**. More on timing: [solar installation timeline](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-installation-timeline-booking-to-net-meter).

## 5 Common Mistakes in Puducherry Applications

1.  **Bill in a late parent's name.** Start the name transfer with the Electricity Department first; the subsidy follows the connection owner.
2.  **System bigger than the sanctioned load.** Apply for a load increase before or with your solar application.
3.  **Choosing an unregistered installer.** Only vendors on the PM Surya Ghar portal can install subsidy systems.
4.  **Non-DCR panels to save money.** They cost you the entire subsidy; get the DCR certificate in writing.
5.  **Assuming the Tamil Nadu ₹22,000 top-up applies.** It does not; ask your installer to confirm the current Puducherry top-up instead.

## Coastal Puducherry: Protect Your Investment

Puducherry has faced strong cyclones such as Thane (2011), Nivar (2020) and Fengal (2024), and salt air rusts weak structures quickly. Insist on:
*   **Hot-dip galvanized structure** with stainless steel fasteners.
*   **Wind-load checked layout** and proper anchoring.
*   **Inverter mounted high**, in shade, away from any water.
*   **Three earthing pits** and a lightning arrester.
