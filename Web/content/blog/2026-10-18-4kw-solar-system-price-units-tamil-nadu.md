**Short answer:** a **4kW solar system makes about 16-20 units a day** in Tamil Nadu (about **960-1,200 units per two-month TNEB bill**). It costs about **₹2.4-2.7 lakh** installed in 2026, or about **₹1.4-1.7 lakh after the up to ₹1 lakh subsidy** for an eligible home. It suits homes using roughly **800-1,100 units every two months**, typically a 3BHK with one or two ACs used moderately.

Because the subsidy stops growing at 3kW, every kW above 3kW is paid fully by you. That is why **4kW makes sense only when your bill really needs it**. If you are between sizes, a correctly sized 3kW plus energy-saving habits can beat an oversized system. ## What Can a 4kW System Run?

A 4kW system's 16-20 units a day can cover a typical day's load like this:

| Appliance | Typical daily units |
| :--- | :--- |
| One 1.5 ton inverter AC, 8 hours | 6 - 8 |
| Second AC, 3-4 hours | 3 - 4 |
| Refrigerator | 1.5 - 2 |
| Fans, lights, TV, Wi-Fi | 2 - 3 |
| Water pump, washing machine, mixer | 1 - 2 |
| **Total** | **about 14 - 19** |

With net metering, units you do not use in the day go to the grid and are credited, so evening AC use is covered by daytime export. See [how many units an AC uses](/blog/2026-04-29-how-many-units-does-ac-consume-per-day-india).

## Single Phase or Three Phase?

Many 4kW systems work on a **single-phase** connection with a 4-5kW single-phase inverter, as long as your **sanctioned load** supports it. If your load is low, TNEB may ask for a load increase first. Larger systems usually move to three phase. Details: [single phase to 3-phase upgrade for solar](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).

For a home using about 1,000 units every two months (a bill of about ₹6,700), a 4kW system can save roughly **₹36,000-40,000 a year**. With a net cost of about ₹1.4-1.7 lakh after subsidy, payback is about **3.5-4.5 years**, followed by 20+ years of low bills. ## 4kW On-Grid or Hybrid?

**On-grid** gives the lowest price and fastest payback, and suits most homes. Choose a **hybrid 4kW system with a battery** only if power cuts are long or frequent in your area, or someone at home needs uninterrupted power. A battery adds roughly ₹1-1.6 lakh for a 5kWh lithium pack and is not subsidised. See [solar with battery backup price](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-with-battery-backup-price-tamil-nadu).

## 3 Common 4kW Sizing Mistakes

1.  **Sizing from one summer bill.** Use the average of your last 6 bills, or you may oversize and pay for units you never use.
2.  **Ignoring a planned AC or EV.** If a second AC or an electric scooter is coming, plan for it now; adding panels later costs more per watt.
3.  **Not checking roof space first.** 4kW needs about 330-400 sq ft of shade-free area. Water tanks and coconut trees can make a 3kW layout the practical maximum. Read [how much roof space solar needs](/blog/2026-10-17-how-much-roof-space-for-solar-panels-sq-ft).

## 4kW Buying Checklist

1.  **DCR panels** in writing, or you lose the subsidy.
2.  **Inverter sized to match**: a 4-5kW on-grid inverter, BIS registered.
3.  **Hot-dip galvanized structure** if you live near the coast.
4.  **Three earthing pits** and a lightning arrester.
5.  **Registered PM Surya Ghar vendor**, with an itemised quote. See [how to compare solar quotations](/blog/2026-10-17-how-to-compare-solar-quotations-checklist).
