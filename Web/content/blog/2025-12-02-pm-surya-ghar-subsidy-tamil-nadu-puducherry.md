**Short answer:** in 2026 a Tamil Nadu home can get **up to ₹1,00,000** in subsidy for rooftop solar. That is **₹78,000** from the central **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** plus a **₹22,000 Tamil Nadu state top-up** (G.O. (Ms) No. 102, Energy Department, 19 September 2026). The full ₹1 lakh applies to systems of **3kW and above** installed by an **empanelled vendor**.

Two clocks are running. The central scheme is approved **till 31 March 2027**, and the state top-up is reported to be limited to the **first 1 lakh eligible homes** (about ₹50 crore). We are already seeing many families in Cuddalore walk in to apply before the money runs out.

> *Rules as of October 2026. Government schemes change; we confirm your exact eligibility on the official portal during the free home visit.*

## Total Subsidy by System Size (Tamil Nadu, 2026)

| System Size | Central PM Surya Ghar | TN State Top-up | **Total Subsidy** | Suits a bill of |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 kW** | ₹30,000 | ₹5,000 | **₹35,000** | Under ₹1,000 / month |
| **2 kW** | ₹60,000 | ₹10,000 | **₹70,000** | ₹1,000 - ₹1,800 / month |
| **3 kW** | ₹78,000 | ₹22,000 | **₹1,00,000** | ₹1,500 - ₹3,500 / month |
| **4 kW, 5 kW and above** | ₹78,000 (max) | ₹22,000 (max) | **₹1,00,000 (max)** | ₹3,500+ / month |

Both subsidies stop growing at 3kW. A 5kW system still gets ₹1 lakh in total; you pay the rest. That is why **3kW is the best value per rupee** for most homes, and 5kW makes sense when your bill is high (two ACs, a big family, or an EV).

### What does a 3kW system cost after ₹1 lakh subsidy?

The typical market price of a 3kW on-grid system in Tamil Nadu is around **₹1,80,000 - ₹2,00,000** before subsidy (it depends on roof, structure height and panel brand). After the full ₹1 lakh comes back to your account, the **real cost is roughly ₹80,000 - ₹1,00,000** for a system that makes about **12-15 units a day** for 25 years. ## Who Is Eligible for the ₹22,000 Tamil Nadu Top-up?

Based on G.O. 102 as reported in October 2026:

*   **Residential homes only.** You need a domestic TNEB / TANGEDCO service connection (Tariff 1A). Commercial, industrial, institutional and agricultural connections are excluded.
*   **Registered on or after 5 August 2026.** Applications made on the PM Surya Ghar portal before this date get the central ₹78,000 but not the state top-up. There is no backdated benefit.
*   **Grid-connected systems.** On-grid systems qualify. Grid-connected hybrid systems (with battery) are reported as eligible; pure off-grid systems are not.
*   **Empanelled vendor only.** The installation must be done by a vendor registered on the PM Surya Ghar portal. If you use an unregistered electrician you can lose **both** subsidies.
*   **First come, first served.** Reports say the state top-up covers the first 1,00,000 eligible applicants, and the budget may run out even earlier.

The central PM Surya Ghar rules also apply: the bill must be in the applicant's name, you must own the house, and the roof must be strong and shade-free.

## Why You Should Not Wait

1.  **The state top-up has a limit.** Once the first 1 lakh homes (or the ₹50 crore budget) are used up, the ₹22,000 is gone.
2.  **PM Surya Ghar is approved till March 2027.** Applications take 4-8 weeks from registration to commissioning, so a home that starts in February is cutting it close.
3.  **Installer queues fill up before deadlines.** Every subsidy deadline brings a rush. Booking your site visit now gets you an earlier installation slot.
4.  **Every month without solar is another full EB bill.** A 3kW system saves roughly ₹2,000 - ₹3,000 a month for a typical Cuddalore home.

7.  **State top-up credited.** TEDA pays the ₹22,000 separately by direct benefit transfer to the same account.

### Documents to keep ready
*   Aadhaar card, with mobile number linked
*   Latest EB bill in the applicant's name
*   Bank passbook or cancelled cheque (same name)
*   Property tax receipt or sale deed
*   Passport-size photo

> **Most common delay:** the name on the EB bill does not match the Aadhaar card. If the meter is still in a parent's name, start the name transfer first. We help with this too.
