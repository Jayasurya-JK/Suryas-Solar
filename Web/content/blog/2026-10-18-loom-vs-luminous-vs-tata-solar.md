**Short answer:** all three are known Indian solar brands, but they are known for **different things**. **Tata Power Solar** is a large panel maker with a **Tamil Nadu factory** and a wide installer network. **Luminous** is best known for **home inverters and batteries**, and also sells solar panels and solar inverters. **Loom Solar** is known for selling **panels and complete kits widely, including online**. For a subsidy home, the brand matters less than **the exact DCR model, the warranty and who installs and services it locally**.

## Questions to Ask Before You Pick Any Brand

1.  **Is this exact model DCR?** Ask for the certificate, not just the brand name.
2.  **What are the product and performance warranty years?** Compare 10 vs 12 vs 15 years product warranty and output at year 25 or 30.
3.  **Who handles warranty claims locally?** A brand with no service partner near you means long waits.
4.  **Which inverter is included, and is it BIS registered?** The inverter matters as much as the panels for day-to-day reliability.
5.  **Is the installer registered on the PM Surya Ghar portal?** Without this, no subsidy.

## Our Honest Take for Tamil Nadu Homes

For a typical 3kW subsidy home, we recommend choosing a **DCR TOPCon panel from a large Indian manufacturer** with a strong local service presence, a **well-supported on-grid inverter**, and a **coastal-grade structure** if you live near the sea. Between brands of similar class, the difference in units per year is small; the difference in installation quality and after-sales service is large. If you also want backup, a hybrid inverter with a solar battery can be added; Luminous and Exide are familiar names for backup products.
