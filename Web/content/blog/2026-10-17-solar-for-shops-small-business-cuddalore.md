**Short answer:** rooftop solar is one of the best investments for **shops, clinics, offices, schools and small workshops in Cuddalore**. Business hours match solar hours, and commercial tariffs have **no free units**, so each solar unit saves a full-price unit. Small commercial systems cost roughly **₹45,000-60,000 per kW** installed in 2026. There is **no PM Surya Ghar subsidy** for businesses, but you can claim **40% accelerated depreciation**, and payback is often **4-6 years** or faster.

> *Prices are market averages as of October 2026, not our quotes. Tax benefits depend on your business structure; confirm with your auditor.*

## Which Businesses Gain the Most?

| Business | Typical daytime load | Suggested size | Why solar fits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Textile, grocery or hardware shop | Lights, fans, 1-2 ACs | 3 - 5kW | Open 9 AM to 9 PM, peak daytime use |
| Medical shop / pharmacy | Fridge, AC, lights | 3 - 5kW | Medicines need steady cooling |
| Clinic or diagnostic lab | ACs, equipment | 5 - 15kW | Heavy daytime load, high bills |
| Office / bank branch | ACs, computers | 5 - 15kW | Business hours = solar hours |
| School or coaching centre | Fans, lights, computers, pumps | 10 - 30kW | Daytime only, long-term savings |
| Hotel, mess or bakery | Fridges, ovens, ACs | 10 - 25kW | Large steady load |
| Small workshop or printing press | Motors, machines | 10 - 30kW | Often three-phase, big bills |

## Tax Benefit: 40% Accelerated Depreciation

Solar plants are treated as renewable energy devices under the Income Tax Act, with **40% depreciation** on the written-down value each year. For a business paying tax, this works like a large deduction in the first years.

| Year | Value at start (₹10 lakh plant) | 40% depreciation | Tax saved at 30% rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | ₹10,00,000 | ₹4,00,000 | ₹1,20,000 |
| 2 | ₹6,00,000 | ₹2,40,000 | ₹72,000 |
| 3 | ₹3,60,000 | ₹1,44,000 | ₹43,200 |

*Simplified example; the first-year rate can differ if the plant is commissioned in the second half of the financial year. Full explanation: [accelerated depreciation for solar](/blog/2026-01-19-commercial-solar-tax-benefits). Always confirm with your chartered accountant.*

## Payback for a Small Business

How fast solar pays back depends on **how much of the solar you use directly** (self-consumption) and your tariff:

*   A shop that is **open all day and uses most solar units itself** gets the fastest payback, often **3-5 years** including the tax benefit.
*   A business that is **closed on Sundays or uses little power at noon** exports more units, so savings depend on the current TNEB metering rules for commercial connections.
*   Clinics, schools and offices with **heavy weekday daytime AC** loads usually fall in the **4-6 year** range.

We calculate payback from your **last 12 months of commercial EB bills**, not from assumptions.

## What TNEB Requires for Commercial Solar

1.  **Connection in the business or owner's name** and the latest paid bill.
2.  **Sanctioned load** that supports the system size; larger systems usually need **three-phase**. See [single phase to 3-phase upgrade](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).
3.  **TNEB approval and the right meter** for the commercial category. Rules for non-domestic solar metering differ from homes and have changed over time, so we confirm the current arrangement for your tariff before you invest.
4.  **Safety:** proper earthing, surge protection and a lightning arrester; see [solar earthing guide](/blog/2026-01-13-solar-earthing-safety-guide).

## Rented Shop? Here Is How It Works

*   Get the **owner's written permission** to use the roof for the agreement period.
*   The EB connection should be **in your name**, or the owner should consent to the solar application.
*   Choose a **removable, bolted structure** so the system can move with you.
*   Agree in writing who owns the system at the end of the lease.

## On-Grid or With Battery?

Most businesses choose **on-grid**: lowest cost and fastest payback. Add a **battery or UPS only for critical loads** like billing computers, CCTV, medical fridges or lab equipment. See [solar with battery backup price](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-with-battery-backup-price-tamil-nadu).

## Cuddalore-Specific Tips

*   **Coastal salt air** around the port, Devanampattinam and Silver Beach areas rusts ordinary GI quickly; insist on **hot-dip galvanized** structures. See [best solar structure for coastal Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-rooftop-solar-structure-types-tamil-nadu).
*   **Shop terraces** often have hoardings, water tanks and neighbouring buildings; get a [shadow analysis](/blog/2026-01-12-solar-shadow-analysis-guide-cuddalore).
*   **Cyclone season (Oct-Dec):** design for wind load and do a pre-monsoon check.
