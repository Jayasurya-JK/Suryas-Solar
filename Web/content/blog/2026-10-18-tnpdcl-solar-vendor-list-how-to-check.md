**Short answer:** registered solar vendors for the Tamil Nadu rooftop subsidy are listed on the **official PM Surya Ghar portal** (pmsuryaghar.gov.in), filtered by **state and district**. You **must** use a vendor from that list: if an **unregistered installer** does the work, you can lose the **full ₹1 lakh subsidy**. But registration only means a vendor is **allowed** to install subsidy systems; you still need to check **quality, office, reviews and warranties** before paying any advance.

> *Portal steps as of October 2026. Screens change; your vendor can show you their registration on the portal.*

## Where to Find the Vendor List

| Where | What you see | Use it to |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PM Surya Ghar portal** (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) | Registered vendors by state and district, with ratings where available | Confirm a company is registered before booking |
| **Your application** on the portal | The vendor selection step | Choose your installer |
| **TNPDCL Unified Solar Rooftop Portal** (tnebltd.gov.in/usrp) | Tamil Nadu application and status | Track your application after choosing a vendor |

## How to Check a Vendor in 5 Minutes

1.  **Ask for the exact name** under which the company is registered on the portal (it can differ from the shop board).
2.  **Search the portal's vendor list** for Tamil Nadu and your district.
3.  **Match the name, district and contact** with the company in front of you.
4.  **Check their Google reviews**: recent reviews with photos of finished installs are a good sign.
5.  **Visit or call their office** and ask how many systems they have installed in your area.

## Registered Is Not Enough: 8 Quality Checks

| Check | Good answer | Warning sign |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Office | Fixed local office you can visit | Only a phone number |
| Panels | Exact DCR model in writing | "Tier-1 panel", no model |
| Structure | Hot-dip galvanized for coastal areas | Painted MS or thin GI |
| Earthing | 3 separate pits + lightning arrester | One pit, no arrester |
| Quote | Itemised, brands named | One lump-sum figure |
| Payment | Moderate advance, balance after installation | 100% upfront |
| Paperwork | They handle TNPDCL and subsidy follow-up | "You do it yourself" |
| Warranty | Panel, inverter and workmanship in writing | Verbal promises |

## Common Questions About the Vendor List

**"Some companies near me are not on the list."** Many electricians and shops sell panels but are not registered for the subsidy. Their systems may work, but you will not get the subsidy through them.

**"Can a vendor from another district install for me?"** Vendors register for areas they serve. Prefer a vendor close enough to service your system quickly after installation, because inverter or wiring faults need a visit.

**"The list shows ratings. Do they matter?"** Ratings help, but also read Google reviews and talk to a past customer.

## What a Registered Vendor Should Do for You

1.  Free site visit, shade check and sizing from your bills.
2.  Written, itemised quote with DCR model and warranties.
3.  Help with portal registration and documents.
4.  Installation with proper structure, cabling and earthing.
5.  Upload of work completion details and panel serials.
6.  Follow-up with TNPDCL for inspection and net meter.
7.  Support until the subsidy is credited, and service afterwards.

## Red Flags Even If a Company Is Registered

*   They ask for **full payment before material arrives**.
*   They avoid naming the **exact panel model** or say "DCR will be arranged later".
*   They promise a **"guaranteed zero bill"** without seeing your bills.
*   They cannot show **any recent installation** or customer you can call.
*   They tell you the **subsidy is assured** before feasibility approval.
