## Brand vs Installer: Which Matters More?

| | Panel brand | Installer |

## 8 Questions to Ask Any Solar Company

1.  Under which name are you registered on the PM Surya Ghar portal?
2.  How many systems have you installed in my district this year?
3.  Which exact panel model will you use, and can I see its DCR certificate?
4.  Which inverter, and what is its warranty and local service arrangement?
5.  What structure do you use near the coast, and how high will it be?
6.  How many earthing pits, and is a lightning arrester included?
7.  Who follows up with TNPDCL for the net meter and the subsidy?
8.  If something fails in year 3, who comes and how fast?

A good company answers all eight clearly and puts the important answers in the quote. If a company avoids naming the panel model or asks for full payment upfront, keep looking.

## What Makes Tamil Nadu Different

*   **Coastal belt** from Chennai to Kanyakumari: salt air needs rust-proof structures.
*   **Northeast monsoon and cyclones** (October to December): wind-load design and good drainage matter.
*   **Heat**: TOPCon panels hold output better on hot afternoons.
*   **TNPDCL process and the ₹22,000 state top-up**: an installer who knows the local section office saves weeks.
