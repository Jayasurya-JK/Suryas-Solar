**Short answer:** **Waaree has the edge on scale and independent reliability testing**: it is India's largest module maker (about **25 GW** of module capacity in early 2026) and its TOPCon panels were named a **Kiwa PVEL "Top Performer" for the fifth year in a row** in 2026. **Premier** is smaller but **makes its own cells**, and its DCR TOPCon panels (up to **620 W, 22.95%**) are very competitive on price and wattage. For a typical Tamil Nadu home, **both are good**. Choose the **DCR model** with the better datasheet, warranty and local service.

## 1. Scale and Track Record

Size is not everything, but for a 25-year product it matters:

*   **Bigger makers** have more service partners, more stock of DCR models and are more likely to be around for warranty claims in 2045.
*   **Waaree's PVEL Top Performer** listing is valuable because it is **independent testing**, not marketing. PVEL stress-tests panels with heat, humidity and load cycles, which is close to what Cuddalore's coastal climate does.
*   **Premier's strength** is making its **own cells**, which helps with DCR supply and quality control, and its newer large-format DCR TOPCon modules.

---

## 2. Efficiency and Heat

Both brands' TOPCon panels are around **22-23% efficient**, and TOPCon handles **heat better than Mono PERC**. On a hot Tamil Nadu terrace that means more units in April and May, when you need them most.

**Glass-to-glass (dual glass)** panels, which Waaree offers in its TOPCon range, can be a good choice within 2-3 km of the sea, because the back glass resists moisture and salt better than a plastic backsheet. Ask for glass-to-glass if you live near the coast.

---

## 3. Subsidy: DCR or Nothing

For the **₹1 lakh subsidy** (₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar + ₹22,000 Tamil Nadu top-up), the panels **must be DCR**. Both brands sell DCR and non-DCR versions that can look identical. Before you pay, ask for:

1.  The **exact model number** and its **DCR certificate**
2.  The **datasheet** (wattage, efficiency, warranty)
3.  **Serial numbers** on the invoice

Details: [₹1 lakh solar subsidy in Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-tamil-nadu-puducherry).

---

## 4. Price

Both brands sell in roughly the same band, **₹22-29 per watt** for modules in 2026. The total system price depends much more on the **structure, inverter, cables, earthing and workmanship**. A typical 3kW DCR system is about **₹1.8 - 2.0 lakh before subsidy** (market average); after the full ₹1 lakh subsidy, roughly **₹80,000 - ₹1,00,000**.
