**Short answer:** in Tamil Nadu, the **physical installation takes only 1-3 days**, but the **whole journey from site visit to net meter and subsidy takes about 6 to 10 weeks**. Most of that time is TNEB paperwork: **feasibility approval (1-2 weeks)** and **inspection plus net meter (2-6 weeks)**. The central subsidy is usually credited **about 30 days after commissioning**. Most delays come from fixable document problems, so prepare before you apply.

## Step 1: Site Visit and Quote (Days 1-3)

An engineer checks your **shade-free roof area**, roof strength, the distance to the meter, earthing locations and your last few EB bills. You get a **system size, layout and written quote**. Bring out:
*   Last 2-3 EB bills
*   Aadhaar card
*   Property tax receipt or sale deed

Not sure of the size? Use the [solar savings calculator](/calc) or check your subsidy first with the [eligibility checker](/subsidy-eligibility).

---

## Step 2: Registration on the PM Surya Ghar Portal (Day 1)

You register with your **EB consumer number** and the mobile number linked to Aadhaar, then apply and choose your **registered vendor**. In Tamil Nadu, applications registered **on or after 5 August 2026** are eligible for the **₹22,000 state top-up** in addition to the central subsidy. Details: [₹1 lakh solar subsidy in Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-tamil-nadu-puducherry).

---

## Step 3: TNEB Feasibility Approval (1-2 Weeks)

The section office checks your connection, **sanctioned load** and whether the local transformer can take the solar capacity. Common hold-ups:
*   **Sanctioned load lower than the system size** (for example 2 kW load for a 3 kW system): a load increase is needed first.
*   **Pending EB dues** on the connection.
*   **Single phase for a large system**: bigger systems may need [a 3-phase upgrade](/blog/2025-12-04-1-phase-to-3-phase-upgrade-solar-tamil-nadu).

---

## Step 4: Installation (1-3 Days)

| System | Typical on-site time |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 - 3kW | 1 - 2 days |
| 5kW | 2 days |
| 10kW | 2 - 4 days |

Work includes the **structure** (hot-dip galvanized for coastal areas), panels, inverter, DC and AC wiring, **three earthing pits** and a lightning arrester. Good installers finish neatly in a day or two and clean up. Read [solar earthing guide](/blog/2026-01-13-solar-earthing-safety-guide).

---

## Step 5-7: Inspection and Net Meter (2-6 Weeks)

After the work completion report is uploaded, TNEB inspects the system and replaces your old meter with a **bi-directional net meter**. This is the step with the most variation; it depends on the section office workload and meter stock. Our detailed guide: [how to get a TNEB net meter in Cuddalore](/blog/2026-01-04-how-to-get-tneb-net-meter-cuddalore-2026).

**Waiting for the net meter?** A [zero export device](/blog/2026-01-18-zero-export-device-guide) can let you use solar for your own daytime load without exporting.

---

## Step 8: Subsidy Credit (About 30 Days)

Once the system is commissioned on the portal, the **central subsidy** is usually credited to your bank account in about **30 days**. The **Tamil Nadu top-up** is paid separately by TEDA through direct benefit transfer. Keep your bank account active and in the same name as the application.

## 6 Things That Delay Solar (and How to Avoid Them)

| Delay | Fix before applying |
| :--- | :--- |
| EB bill name differs from Aadhaar | Start the name transfer first |
| Sanctioned load too low | Apply for load increase with the solar application |
| Pending EB dues | Clear them before registering |
| Wrong mobile number on Aadhaar | Update so OTPs reach you |
| Bank account name mismatch | Use an account in the applicant's name |
| Choosing an unregistered installer | Pick a vendor registered on the PM Surya Ghar portal |

More reasons applications get stuck: [PM Surya Ghar application rejected? 8 reasons and fixes](/blog/2026-10-17-pm-surya-ghar-application-rejected-reasons-fixes).

The central scheme is approved till **31 March 2027**, and the Tamil Nadu top-up is reported to be limited to the **first 1 lakh eligible homes**.
