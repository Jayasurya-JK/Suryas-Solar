**Short answer:** **DCR means Domestic Content Requirement**: a DCR solar panel uses **solar cells made in India**, assembled into modules in India. **DCR panels are compulsory for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy** and the Tamil Nadu top-up, so if your installer uses non-DCR panels, you can lose **the full ₹1 lakh**. DCR panels cost more per watt, but for a subsidy home the subsidy covers the difference many times over. Always get the **model number, DCR certificate and panel serial numbers in writing**.

## DCR vs Non-DCR at a Glance

| | DCR panel | Non-DCR panel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Solar cells | Made in India | Usually imported |
| Module assembly | India | India or abroad |
| PM Surya Ghar subsidy | **Eligible** | **Not eligible** |
| Tamil Nadu ₹22,000 top-up | Eligible (with central subsidy) | Not eligible |
| Typical price (2026) | about ₹22 - 29 per watt | about ₹14 - 20 per watt |
| Best for | Homes claiming subsidy | Businesses not claiming subsidy |
| Performance | Same technologies (Mono PERC, TOPCon) | Same technologies |

**Important:** DCR is about **where the cells are made**, not about quality. A DCR TOPCon panel and a non-DCR TOPCon panel can perform the same. The reason DCR matters for you is the **subsidy rule**.

## Why the Subsidy Needs DCR

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana supports Indian solar manufacturing, so it pays the subsidy only for systems built with Indian-made cells. During inspection, the panel details and serial numbers are checked against the system registered on the portal. If they are non-DCR, the subsidy claim fails. Full subsidy rules: [₹1 lakh solar subsidy in Tamil Nadu](/blog/2025-12-02-pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-tamil-nadu-puducherry).

**The maths for a 3kW home:**

| | DCR panels | Non-DCR panels |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Panel cost (3kW) | about ₹66,000 - ₹87,000 | about ₹42,000 - ₹60,000 |
| Subsidy (eligible TN home) | up to ₹1,00,000 | ₹0 |
| **Net effect** | You save far more | You "save" ₹25,000 and lose ₹1 lakh |

## What Is ALMM?

**ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers)** is a list maintained by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE):
*   **List-I:** approved solar **module** makers and models.
*   **List-II:** approved solar **cell** makers.

Panels used in government-supported projects must come from listed models. For a subsidy home, look for a **DCR model from an ALMM-listed manufacturer**. Most big Indian brands (Tata Power Solar, Adani, Waaree, Premier, Exide and others) sell DCR models; not every model they sell is DCR, so check the exact model. Brand comparisons: [Waaree vs Adani vs Tata](/blog/2026-10-17-waaree-vs-adani-vs-tata-solar-panel-comparison).

## 6 Ways to Check Your Panels Are Genuine DCR

1.  **Model number in the quote.** "Tier-1 DCR panel" is not enough. Get the exact model.
2.  **DCR certificate.** The manufacturer issues a certificate for DCR supply; ask your installer for a copy.
3.  **Label on every panel.** Check the model on the back label matches the quote and datasheet.
4.  **Serial numbers on the invoice.** Every panel's serial number should appear on your invoice; they are needed for the subsidy upload.
5.  **Portal acceptance.** At inspection, the serials are verified against the PM Surya Ghar application. Ask your installer to show you the uploaded details.
6.  **Price sanity check.** A "DCR" system priced far below the market (about ₹1.8-2.1 lakh for 3kW in 2026) is a warning sign.

## When Non-DCR Panels Make Sense

*   **Shops, offices and factories** that do not claim PM Surya Ghar subsidy can use ALMM-listed **non-DCR** panels and save on cost. They benefit from accelerated depreciation instead: [solar for shops and small businesses](/blog/2026-10-17-solar-for-shops-small-business-cuddalore).
*   **Homes not claiming subsidy** (for example, a second system on the same connection) can also consider non-DCR, but compare the total cost carefully.

For every subsidy home, the answer is simple: **DCR only**.

## DCR Myths We Hear Every Week

*   **"DCR panels are lower quality."** No. DCR is about where cells are made. Many DCR panels use the same TOPCon technology as imported ones.
*   **"Any Indian brand is DCR."** No. A brand can sell both DCR and non-DCR models. Only the exact model and certificate count.
*   **"The installer can change to DCR later."** Panels are verified at inspection; replacing them after installation is expensive and delays your subsidy.
