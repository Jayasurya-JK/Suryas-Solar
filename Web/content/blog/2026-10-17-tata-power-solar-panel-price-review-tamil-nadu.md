## Why Local Manufacturing Matters for Tamil Nadu Buyers

1.  **DCR supply.** The subsidy needs DCR panels. A large DCR line in our own state means better availability, even during deadline rushes.
2.  **Climate familiarity.** Panels built and tested in Tamil Nadu are made for heat and humidity, though coastal homes still need a proper galvanized structure.
3.  **Faster service.** Warranty replacements and technical support move faster when the factory and the service network are in the same state.

## How to Make Sure You Get Genuine Tata DCR Panels

1.  Ask for the **exact model number** and its **DCR certificate**.
2.  Match the **label on each panel** with the datasheet and the invoice.
3.  Make sure the invoice lists the **panel serial numbers** (needed for the subsidy upload).
4.  Buy from an **authorised partner** who can support warranty claims locally.
5.  Be careful with prices far below the market; non-DCR panels will **cost you the entire subsidy**. See [solar scam warnings](/blog/2026-01-09-solar-scam-warnings-cuddalore).

## Questions to Ask Before Buying a Tata Solar System

1.  Is this a **TOPCon or Mono PERC** model, and what is the wattage per panel?
2.  Is the model **DCR**, and will the certificate and serial numbers come with the invoice?
3.  What are the **product and performance warranty** terms on this exact model?
4.  Which **inverter** is included, and what is its warranty?
5.  Are you an **authorised partner**, and who handles warranty claims locally?
6.  Does the price include **structure, earthing, net meter process and subsidy filing**?

A good installer answers all six in writing before you pay.
